Questions, answered

Everything you need to know.

From artwork and samples to delivery, here are the answers that make ordering branded merch feel simple.

What is the minimum order quantity?

Minimum quantities vary by product. Many popular items start from just 25 units, while selected products are available in even smaller runs.

Can you help with artwork and design?

Yes. Our artwork team can prepare your logo, check colours and create a production-ready proof before anything goes to print.

Can I see a sample before placing a full order?

Absolutely. Samples are available for most products so you can review quality, colour and sizing before confirming your order.

How long will my merch take to arrive?

Standard lead times are typically two to four weeks after artwork approval. Your account team will confirm timing for every order.

Do you deliver Australia-wide?

Yes. We deliver to metro, regional and remote locations across Australia, including split deliveries to multiple offices or events.
